Product

The StarNav platform

A modular autonomy system for teams building robots that need to navigate, execute missions, and improve after deployment.

01

Robot Runtime

StarNav runs on the robot to support perception, localization, obstacle awareness, mission-aware planning, and safe recovery behaviors.

  • Sensor fusion and map alignment
  • Route planning and local obstacle response
  • Operator handoff and recovery behavior
02

Validation Layer

Teams can validate autonomy behavior before field rollout through repeatable scenarios, regression checks, and deployment gates.

  • Scenario libraries for target environments
  • Robot-specific policy adaptation
  • Release checks before field exposure
03

Fleet Operations

StarNav connects field robots back to operators with mission visibility, diagnostics, telemetry, and update workflows.

  • Mission dashboards and health monitoring
  • Remote diagnostics and version tracking
  • Feedback loops from field data
