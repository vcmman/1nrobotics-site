About 1N Robotics

We are building the autonomy layer for real robot deployment.

1N Robotics exists to help robots navigate intelligently and autonomously, regardless of form factor or field environment.

Mission

Make capable robots easier to deploy.

Every robotics team has to bridge the same hard gap: a robot that works in a controlled demo must become a robot that can operate reliably around messy real-world conditions.

StarNav combines autonomy software, validation workflows, and fleet operations tooling so teams can spend less time rebuilding navigation infrastructure and more time shipping differentiated robots.

Production-minded

We care about telemetry, validation, fallback behavior, and rollout discipline because deployment is where autonomy becomes real.

Robot-agnostic

StarNav is designed around the shared autonomy patterns across drones, mobile robots, quadrupeds, and humanoids.

Built with teams

We work with robotics builders who already have hardware ambition and need a stronger path to field-ready navigation.
